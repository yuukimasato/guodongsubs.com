## Synopsis

In this world, Outcasts exist. Zhang Chulan is an Outcast hiding among the ordinary; for the first nineteen years of his life, he had carefully hidden the differences between him and the others. One day, a mysterious girl Feng Baobao came for him, and he soon found himself chased by Jiangshis, attacked by weirdos, and embroiled in chaos never seen before...
